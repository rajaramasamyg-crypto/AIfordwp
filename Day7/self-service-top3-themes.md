# Self-Service Answers – Top 3 Migration Issues

---

## 1. Can't Sign In / Account Locked Out

**Try this first:**
1. Wait 15 minutes and try signing in again — your account may unlock on its own.
2. If you know your password is correct but it still won't accept it, use the self-service password reset at [aka.ms/sspr](https://aka.ms/sspr) to unlock your account and set a new password.

**Still not working? Contact the Service Desk and tell them:**
- Your name and username
- The exact error message you see on screen
- How many times you have tried to sign in today

---

## 2. OneDrive Files Appear Missing

**Try this first:**
1. Open OneDrive and click the sync icon in the taskbar (bottom right). Wait two minutes for it to finish syncing.
2. Check your OneDrive Recycle Bin at [onedrive.com](https://onedrive.com) — deleted files are kept there for up to 30 days.

**Still not working? Contact the Service Desk and tell them:**
- Your name and the names of any files or folders you cannot find
- Whether the files were there before this week's upgrade

---

## 3. Can't Access the S: Drive (Finance Shared Drive)

**Try this first:**
1. Sign out of your computer completely and sign back in — this refreshes your access permissions.
2. If you still see "Access Denied", do not try anything else as this needs IT to fix your permissions directly.

**Contact the Service Desk and tell them:**
- Your name and username
- That you cannot access S:/Finance and are seeing "Access Denied"
- What you need the drive for (e.g. month-end reports) so they can prioritise
