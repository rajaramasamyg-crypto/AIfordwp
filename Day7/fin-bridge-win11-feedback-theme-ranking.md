The **Cosmetic UI/Personalisation** theme has the highest raw count (9 comments) and **Positive Feedback** also has 9. Ranking by count alone would put cosmetic issues at #1 — a mistake. These are Minor/Positive and require zero urgent action today. The three Blocker themes above each represent users who **cannot do their jobs**, and that always outranks aesthetics regardless of volume.

Similarly, **Floor 3 Printer Mapping** (6 Friction) has a higher count than Rank 3, but printer issues have workarounds (email documents, use another floor) whereas S: drive denial does not — users are hard-blocked on time-sensitive financial deadlines.
