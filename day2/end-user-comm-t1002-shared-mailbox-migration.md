# End-User Communication — T-1002: Unable to Open Shared Mailbox After Migration

**Date:** 2026-08-04  
**Ticket:** T-1002  
**To:** Affected Finance User  
**From:** DWP Service Desk  
**Subject:** Shared Mailbox Access — We Are Looking Into This

---

Hi,

Thank you for getting in touch. We are sorry to hear you cannot access the shared Finance mailbox in Outlook following the recent email system migration.

**What has happened?**  
During the migration, the shared mailbox was moved to the new email system. Unfortunately, the permissions that previously gave you access to this mailbox do not always transfer automatically — this means your account may need to be re-granted access in the new system. This is a known behaviour following this type of migration and is straightforward to fix.

Your emails in the shared mailbox are safe — nothing has been lost.

**What are we doing about it?**  
Our team is checking your access permissions in the new email system now. Once confirmed, we will re-grant your access to the shared mailbox and make sure it appears correctly in your Outlook.

**What can you do in the meantime?**

1. **Check your own mailbox** — your personal emails should be unaffected. If you are also having issues with your own Outlook, please let us know.
2. **Ask a colleague** — if a colleague in Finance still has access to the shared mailbox, they may be able to handle any urgent emails while we get yours fixed.
3. **Do not create a new Outlook profile yourself** — please wait for our team to resolve this to avoid any further complications.

**What happens next?**  
We will contact you as soon as your access has been restored, and we will ask you to confirm that the shared mailbox is visible and working correctly in Outlook.

If the matter is urgent, please call the Service Desk on the usual number and quote ticket number **T-1002**.
