# End-User Communication — T-1008: VPN Connected but Cannot Access Work Systems

**Date:** 2026-08-04  
**Ticket:** T-1008  
**To:** Affected User  
**From:** DWP Service Desk  
**Subject:** VPN Issue — Connected but Unable to Reach Internal Systems

---

Hi,

Thank you for getting in touch. We know this is a serious issue — being connected to VPN but still unable to reach any of your work systems means you are effectively unable to work remotely, and we are treating this as high priority.

**What has happened?**  
Your VPN is showing as connected, but internal resources — such as shared drives, the intranet, and internal applications — are not reachable. This started after your Windows 11 upgrade. What has likely happened is that the upgrade changed some network settings on your computer, which means that even though the VPN tunnel is open, your computer is not correctly routing your work traffic through it. This is a known issue that can happen after an operating system upgrade and is fixable.

**What are we doing about it?**  
Our team will connect to your device remotely to check the network configuration and VPN settings. We will correct the routing and firewall settings that the upgrade may have changed so that your VPN works properly again.

**Things you can try right now:**

1. **Disconnect and reconnect the VPN** — close the VPN application fully, wait 30 seconds, reopen it, and reconnect. Sometimes this alone can refresh the connection.
2. **Restart your computer** — after restarting, reconnect to the VPN and try to access an internal resource again.
3. **Try running the VPN as Administrator** — right-click the VPN application icon and select **Run as administrator**, then connect. If this works, it points to a permissions issue we can resolve.

**What happens next?**  
If the steps above do not resolve the issue, a member of our team will contact you to connect remotely and apply the fix. Please keep your computer on and connected to the internet so we can reach you.

If you have a critical deadline and cannot wait, please call the Service Desk directly on the usual number and quote ticket number **T-1008** — we will prioritise your call.
