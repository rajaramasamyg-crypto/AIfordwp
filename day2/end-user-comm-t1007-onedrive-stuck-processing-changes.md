# End-User Communication — T-1007: OneDrive Stuck and Files Appearing Missing

**Date:** 2026-08-04  
**Ticket:** T-1007  
**To:** Affected User  
**From:** DWP Service Desk  
**Subject:** Your OneDrive — Files and Sync Issue After Migration

---

Hi,

Thank you for raising this with us. We completely understand the concern when files appear to be missing, and we want to reassure you straight away: **your files are almost certainly safe**.

**What has happened?**  
Since the recent migration, your OneDrive application on your computer is showing "processing changes" and some files that used to be on your computer locally are no longer visible. This has happened because the migration moved your files to a new account or location in the cloud, but the OneDrive app on your computer is still connected to the old one. It does not know where to look for your files yet — but your files are still there, stored safely online.

**What are we doing about it?**  
Our team is checking the status of your files online right now to confirm they are intact. We will then reconnect your OneDrive app to your new account so that your files sync back to your computer correctly.

**Important — please do not do the following until we contact you:**
- Do not delete any files from your OneDrive folder on your computer
- Do not uninstall OneDrive yourself
- Do not sign out of OneDrive without guidance from our team

**How to check your files are safe right now:**  
You can check your files immediately using a web browser:
1. Open your internet browser (e.g. Edge or Chrome)
2. Go to [https://www.office.com](https://www.office.com) and sign in with your work email address and password
3. Click on **OneDrive** from the list of apps
4. Your files should be visible here — this confirms they are safe in the cloud even if they are not showing on your computer

**What happens next?**  
Our team will contact you shortly to reconnect your OneDrive to the new account. This process should not result in any data loss, and we will guide you through it step by step if needed.

If your files are not visible on the Office 365 website either, please call us immediately on the usual number and quote ticket number **T-1007** — we will treat this as urgent.
