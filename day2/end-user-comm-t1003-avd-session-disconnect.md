# End-User Communication — T-1003: AVD Session Keeps Disconnecting

**Date:** 2026-08-04

**To:** Affected User  
**From:** DWP Service Desk  
**Subject:** Your Virtual Desktop — Repeated Disconnections

---

Hi,

Thank you for reporting this. We can see from your ticket that your virtual desktop session is dropping and reconnecting roughly every 10 minutes, and we understand how disruptive that is to your work.

**What has happened?**  
Your virtual desktop session is disconnecting at regular intervals and then reconnecting. The good news is that your session is coming back each time, so you are not locked out. This kind of regular, timed disconnection is often caused by a session timeout setting — essentially a rule that automatically pauses inactive sessions — or by a network-level issue that briefly interrupts the connection.

**What are we doing about it?**  
We have raised this with our platform team to check the session settings on your virtual desktop environment. We will also check whether the disconnections happen during inactive periods or during active use, as this helps us pinpoint the cause.

**Things you can try right now:**

1. **Stay active during the session** — if the disconnections mainly happen when you have stepped away, try moving the mouse or pressing a key periodically. This may help while we investigate.
2. **Check your internet connection** — if you are working from home, try restarting your router. Unstable Wi-Fi can cause these types of disconnections.
3. **Note the timing** — if possible, make a note of whether the disconnection happens after 10 minutes of being away from the screen, or during active use. This will help us diagnose the cause faster.

**What happens next?**  
Our team will investigate the session settings and get back to you with an update. If the issue is resolved by a settings change on our end, you will not need to do anything — the disconnections should simply stop.

If you lose any work as a result of a disconnection, please let us know and quote ticket number **T-1003**.
