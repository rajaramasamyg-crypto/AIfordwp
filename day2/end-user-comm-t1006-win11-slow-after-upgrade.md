# End-User Communication — T-1006: Computer Running Slowly After Windows 11 Upgrade

**Subject:** Your Computer Running Slowly — What Is Happening and What to Expect

---

Hi,

Thank you for getting in touch. We understand how frustrating it is when your computer feels sluggish, especially after what should have been an improvement.

**What has happened?**  
Your computer was upgraded to Windows 11 approximately two days ago, and since then everything has been running slowly. This is actually a very common experience in the days following a Windows upgrade. After an upgrade, Windows runs several background tasks — updating security definitions, indexing files so searches work faster, and downloading any remaining updates — all of which use up processing power and can make everything else feel slow.

The good news is that in most cases, the slowness is temporary and things should improve significantly over the next day or two as these background tasks complete.

**What are we doing about it?**  
We have logged your ticket and our team will check whether there is anything specific on your device that is causing the slow performance beyond the usual post-upgrade activity. We may connect to your device remotely to take a look.

**Things you can do right now:**

1. **Leave your computer on and connected overnight** — this allows all the background update and maintenance tasks to complete without interrupting your work.
2. **Avoid switching it off mid-task** — shutting down while background processes are running can make them start again from scratch the next time you log in.
3. **Give it until tomorrow** — if the slowness is caused by background tasks, your computer should feel noticeably faster by tomorrow morning.
4. **Let us know if it does not improve** — if the computer is still slow after the weekend, please contact us again. It may mean a driver needs updating or a hardware check is required.

**What happens next?**  
If we identify anything that needs to be fixed on your device, we will contact you to arrange a remote session. Otherwise, the performance should improve on its own.

Please quote ticket number **T-1006** if you need to follow up with us.
