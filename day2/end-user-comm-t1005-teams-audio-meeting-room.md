# End-User Communication — T-1005: Teams Audio Not Working in Meeting Room

**Date:** 2026-08-04  
**Ticket:** T-1005  
**To:** Meeting Room Users — 3 Affected  
**From:** DWP Service Desk  
**Subject:** Teams Audio Issue in Your Meeting Room

---

Hi,

Thank you for reporting this. We are aware that the audio in Microsoft Teams is not working on the machines in your meeting room, and we are treating this as a high-priority issue.

**What has happened?**  
The microphone and speakers are not working in Teams on the three computers in your meeting room. Because all three machines are affected at the same time and in the same room, it is most likely that a shared audio device in the room (such as a speakerphone or conference hub) has lost its connection, lost power, or needs to be reset. It is less likely to be a problem with three separate computers.

**What are we doing about it?**  
Our team has been alerted and will attend to the meeting room as soon as possible to investigate and resolve the fault.

**Things you can try right now:**

1. **Check the room audio device** — if there is a shared speakerphone or conference device in the room (it may look like a puck, a hub, or a bar), check that it is switched on and that its cable is firmly plugged in. If it has a power light, check it is lit.
2. **Unplug and replug the audio device** — if it is connected via USB, try unplugging the cable, waiting 10 seconds, and plugging it back in. Wait another 30 seconds and then check Teams.
3. **Check Teams audio settings** — in Teams, click your profile picture, then go to **Settings > Devices**. Check that the correct microphone and speaker are selected. If it says "Windows Default," try selecting the room device from the list instead.
4. **Use a headset as a temporary workaround** — if you have a USB or 3.5mm headset available, plug it in and select it in Teams Settings > Devices to continue with any calls until the room issue is fixed.

**What happens next?**  
An engineer will visit the room to resolve the issue. If you have a meeting scheduled soon and need urgent help, please call the Service Desk immediately on the usual number and quote ticket number **T-1005**.
