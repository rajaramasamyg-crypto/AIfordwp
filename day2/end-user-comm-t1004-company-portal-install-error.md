# End-User Communication — T-1004: App Not Installing from Company Portal

**Date:** 2026-08-04  
**Ticket:** T-1004  
**To:** Affected User  
**From:** DWP Service Desk  
**Subject:** Company Portal App Installation — We Are Looking Into This

---

Hi,

Thank you for letting us know. We are sorry the application you need is not installing correctly through the Company Portal.

**What has happened?**  
When you tried to install the application through the Company Portal, it showed an error and did not complete. The error code we have recorded (0x87D1041C) tells us that the installation process ran, but the system was unable to confirm it finished correctly. This is usually caused by a missing software component on your device, a syncing issue between your device and our management system, or a small problem with how the app has been packaged for deployment.

This is not something you have done wrong.

**What are we doing about it?**  
Our team is checking the installation logs on your device and reviewing the app status in our management system. We will identify exactly what caused the failure and either fix it remotely or push the application to your device again.

**Things you can try right now:**

1. **Sync your device** — open the Company Portal app, go to **Settings**, and tap or click **Sync**. Wait a few minutes and then try installing the app again.
2. **Restart your device** — a full restart can clear temporary issues that prevent installations from completing. Once restarted, try the Company Portal again.
3. **Try the installation again** — after syncing and restarting, attempt to install the application one more time from the Company Portal.

**What happens next?**  
If the steps above do not resolve it, please let us know and our team will investigate further. We may need to log in to your device remotely to check the installation logs and push the fix directly.

Please quote ticket number **T-1004** if you call the Service Desk.
