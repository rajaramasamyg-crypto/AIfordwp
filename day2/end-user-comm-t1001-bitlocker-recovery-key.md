# End-User Communication — T-1001: BitLocker Recovery Key Prompt on Every Boot

**Date:** 2026-08-04  
**Ticket:** T-1001  
**To:** Affected User  
**From:** DWP Service Desk  
**Subject:** Your New Laptop — Security Screen Appearing at Startup

---

Hi,

Thank you for reporting this issue. We understand how disruptive it is to face a security screen every time you start your laptop, especially on a brand-new device.

**What has happened?**  
Your laptop is showing a screen asking for a long recovery key every time it starts up. This is a security feature called BitLocker, which is designed to protect the data on your device. Normally, your laptop should handle this automatically in the background — the fact that it is asking you for a key every time means something in the initial setup of the device needs to be corrected.

Your data is safe, and this is not a sign that anything malicious has happened. It is a configuration issue that we can fix.

**What are we doing about it?**  
We have logged this as a high-priority ticket. Our team will review the setup of your device and apply the correct configuration so that the security check runs silently in the background without interrupting you at startup.

**What do you need to do right now?**

1. **Do not worry about your files** — your data is secure.
2. **Keep note of your recovery key** — if you have been entering a recovery key to log in, please keep it somewhere safe until we confirm the issue is resolved. Do not share it with anyone other than the Service Desk.
3. **Do not attempt to reset or reinstall Windows** — please wait for us to look at this before making any changes to the device.

**What happens next?**  
An engineer will contact you to arrange a time to look at your device remotely or in person. We aim to have this resolved without any disruption to your work or loss of data.

If you have any questions in the meantime, please call the Service Desk on the usual number and quote ticket number **T-1001**.
