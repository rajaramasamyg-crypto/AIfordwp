# Floor 6 Partner Update (By Lunch)

Version: 1.0  
Date: 2026-08-14  
Audience: Partners

This morning, Floor 6 Legal had a service disruption: many people could not sign in, and others saw very long login times.

What happened:
- A core login service path for Floor 6 became unstable after weekend system changes.
- That reduced available session capacity, which caused the failed and delayed sign-ins.

What we are doing:
- We applied the recovery runbook on the affected controller.
- We restarted and stabilized the service path.
- We are now monitoring recovery and validating with affected users.

What is already improving:
- Registration capacity is recovering.
- Login testing with impacted users is in progress.
- Priority users are being handled first so legal work can continue.

What is still open:
- Final confirmation that all affected users are fully back to normal.
- Monitoring period completion to confirm the issue does not return.

What you can expect next:
- A follow-up update after verification completes.
- A confirmed closure time once recovery remains stable.
