# L1 Self-Service - Floor 6 Login Is Slow or Fails

Version: 1.0  
Date: 2026-08-14  
Derived From: Runbook-Floor6-Login-Recovery-Section4-v1.0

If your Floor 6 sign-in is failing or taking a very long time, use this quick path:

1. Restart your laptop once, then try again.
2. If it still fails or is very slow, raise a ticket and include:
- Your name and team
- Device name
- Time of last failed login
- A screenshot of the error, if shown
3. If possible, try one more login after 15 minutes.
4. Do not repeat sign-in attempts continuously, as this can slow recovery checks.

What IT is doing now:
- Repairing the Floor 6 login service path
- Bringing session capacity back to normal
- Testing with affected users before full close

When to expect improvement:
- Most users recover after the service restart and registration refresh window.

If you are blocked on urgent client work, mark your ticket as business critical so the desk can prioritize your access route.
