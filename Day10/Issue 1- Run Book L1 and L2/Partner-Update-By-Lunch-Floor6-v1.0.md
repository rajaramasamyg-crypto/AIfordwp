# Floor 6 Update For Partners

Version: 1.0  
Date: 2026-08-14  
Audience: Partners and practice leadership

This morning, Floor 6 had two linked problems: people were struggling to sign in, and one person saw a case document in Copilot that looked outside her normal work.

What happened in plain terms:
- A rollout setting was applied too broadly.
- That broader setting created sign-in friction for part of Floor 6.
- It also exposed an existing access-setting weakness in our document estate.

What we are doing now:
- We have stopped the spread by removing affected people and devices from the rollout path.
- We are checking and correcting access settings for the impacted group.
- Security and Legal are jointly reviewing the document-visibility event.

What is already true:
- This is contained to a defined group and being handled as a priority.
- We have a documented technical rollback path in execution.
- We are preserving evidence and audit records for full review.

What is still open:
- Final confirmation that all impacted users are fully restored.
- Full check for any similar access issues in related document areas.
- Final timeline for complete closeout, which depends on verification results.

Next update:
- A status update will be sent after verification checks complete.
