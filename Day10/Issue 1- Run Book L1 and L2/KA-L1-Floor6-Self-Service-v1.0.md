# L1 Knowledge Article - Floor 6 Login/Copilot Quick Help

Version: 1.0  
Date: 2026-08-14  
Source: Runbook-Floor6-Ring-Containment-and-Rollback-v1.0

If your Floor 6 laptop is taking a long time to sign in, or Copilot shows a file that looks unrelated to your work, follow these steps:

1. Restart once and try sign-in again.
2. If sign-in is still slow or fails, submit a service ticket with:
   - Your device name
   - Time of the last failed sign-in
   - Screenshot of the error (if any)
3. If Copilot showed a document that seems out of scope, do not open, copy, or share it. Report it in the same ticket.

What IT is doing:
- Removing affected devices from a faulty rollout group
- Rechecking account access settings
- Syncing corrected settings back to devices

What you can expect:
- Some users recover quickly after policy sync
- You may be asked to sign out/sign back in once IT confirms your fix

This issue is being handled as a priority with Security and Legal oversight.
