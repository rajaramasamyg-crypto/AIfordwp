# Partner Update by Lunch - Floor 6 Legal

Version: 1.0  
Date: 2026-08-14  
Audience: Legal partners and non-technical stakeholders

## What happened
After a new document management tool was rolled out on Friday, some Floor 6 users lost desktop shortcuts. This made it look like apps were gone even though the underlying files and systems are still there.

## What we are doing now
1. We have paused the rollout to stop further impact.
2. We are restoring shortcuts on affected devices first.
3. We are checking a sample of devices and users before wider re-enablement.
4. Security and IT are jointly reviewing the rollout settings before restart.

## What this means for your teams
- Documents are not deleted.
- This is primarily an access and desktop experience issue.
- Priority support is in place for blocked users.

## What is still open
- Final confirmation of the exact deployment step that removed shortcuts.
- Final decision on revised rollout scope and timing.

## Next update
You will receive the next update after verification is complete on the first recovery wave today.
