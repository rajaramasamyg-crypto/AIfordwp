# KA L1 - Floor 6 Shortcut Recovery (Self-Service)

Version: 1.0  
Date: 2026-08-14  
Audience: Floor 6 Legal users

If your desktop shortcuts disappeared, this is a known issue from a recent software rollout and is being fixed now.

What to do now:
1. Restart your device once.
2. After sign-in, check Desktop and Public Desktop for FinBridge DMS shortcut.
3. If shortcut is still missing, open Company Portal and click Sync.
4. Wait 10 minutes and check again.
5. If still missing, raise a ticket with:
   - Device name
   - Time issue started
   - Whether other icons also disappeared

What not to do:
- Do not reinstall the app yourself.
- Do not delete remaining shortcuts.

What we are doing:
- We paused the Floor 6 rollout scope.
- We are restoring approved shortcuts in a controlled way.
- We will confirm once all affected users are validated.

Your files are safe. This issue affects shortcut visibility, not document storage.
