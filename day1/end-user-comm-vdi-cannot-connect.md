**From:** DWP Service Desk  
**Subject:** Your Virtual Desktop (VDI) — Unable to Connect

---

Hi,

Thank you for getting in touch with us. We have received your report that you are unable to connect to your virtual desktop (VDI) this morning and are unable to work as a result.

**What has happened?**  
When you try to connect to your virtual desktop from home, you are seeing a "cannot connect" error. This type of issue can be caused by a few different things — a problem with your internet connection, a temporary fault on the VDI system, or a change that happened over the weekend.

**What are we doing about it?**  
We have logged your ticket and our team is looking into this now. The first thing we will check is whether there is a wider issue affecting the VDI service, and if not, we will work through the most likely causes specific to your setup.

**Things you can try right now:**

1. **Restart the VDI app** — close the virtual desktop application fully and reopen it, then try connecting again.
2. **Restart your device** — switch your computer off completely, wait 30 seconds, and turn it back on. Then try connecting to your virtual desktop again.
3. **Check your internet connection** — make sure you can browse normal websites. If your broadband is down, that would explain why the virtual desktop cannot connect.
4. **Check for a service alert** — if you can access the DWP staff intranet or service status page from another device, check whether a known issue has already been reported.

**What happens next?**  
A member of our team will be in touch shortly to work through the issue with you. If you have not heard from us within the hour, please call the Service Desk on the usual number.

Apologies for the disruption to your working day.
