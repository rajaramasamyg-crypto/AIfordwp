# L1 Self-Service: Finance Team Access Issue

v 1.0, 10/08/2026, status: Draft

## What is happening
You may see an error when trying to open a shared drive such as your S: drive or a Finance folder on the network. This can happen for several reasons and the support team is working to identify the cause.

## Your files are safe
Your documents and files stored on the shared drive have not been deleted. The files are still on the server. You are simply unable to reach them right now.

## What you can do now
1. Check whether the drive letter is still visible in File Explorer (for example S: or F:).
2. If the drive appears but shows an error when you open it, note the exact error message. You will need it when you contact the help desk.
3. Try logging off your computer completely and logging back in, then attempt the drive again.
4. If the drive has disappeared from File Explorer, try restarting your computer once, then log back in.
5. After restarting, wait two minutes before attempting the drive again to allow the network connection to fully re-establish.
6. If you are working remotely, check that your VPN is connected and showing a green or connected status before trying the drive.
7. If the drive still does not open after the steps above, stop and contact the IT help desk. Do not attempt further changes to your account or computer.

## Before you contact the IT help desk
Please have the following ready so support can help you faster:
- Your full work email address.
- The drive letter or folder path you are trying to reach (for example: S: or \\\\server\\Finance\\Shared).
- The exact error message shown on screen (copy the text or attach a screenshot).
- The time the problem started.
- Your device name (usually shown on a sticker on your laptop or found in Settings > System > About).
- Whether colleagues in your team are having the same problem or if it is just you.
- Whether you recently had a password reset, a new device, or any changes made to your account.

## What support will do
The support team will check your account, group access settings, and the file server to find the cause. They will restore your access and confirm with you that the drive is working before closing the request.

Contact the IT help desk immediately if you are unable to open any Finance files and this is blocking urgent or time-sensitive work.
