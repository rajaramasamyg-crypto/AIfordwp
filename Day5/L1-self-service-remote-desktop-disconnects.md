# L1 Self-Service: Company Work Screen Keeps Closing

v 1.0, 07/08/2026, status : Draft

## What is happening
If your company work screen opens and then closes, that is frustrating. You are not alone, and this is usually temporary.

## Your files are safe
Your files in company storage, shared folders, OneDrive, Outlook, and Teams are safe. When the screen closes, your documents are not deleted.

## What you can do now
1. Close the company work screen app or browser tab completely.
2. Wait 2 to 3 minutes.
3. Open it again and log in.
4. If it closes again, log out of the app.
5. Wait 5 minutes, then try one more time.
6. If you can, use a web browser instead of the app for now.
7. If it still keeps closing, stop trying and contact the IT help desk.

## Before you contact the IT help desk
Please gather these details so support can help faster:
- Your full work email address.
- The time it last closed (include your local time).
- What device you are using (for example: company laptop, home computer, phone).
- Whether it closes right after log in or after a few minutes.
- The exact message shown (copy the text or attach a screenshot).
- Whether this affects only you or other teammates too.
- What you used to connect (app name or browser name).

## What support will do
The support team will connect you through a working route and keep checking that it stays connected. You do not need to do technical fix steps yourself.

Contact the IT help desk if it closes more than once in 10 minutes, you cannot reconnect after the steps above, or urgent work is blocked.
